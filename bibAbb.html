Authors and Works Cited

The works Liebermann cites in the Wörterbuch other than his own Gesetze (Toller, Sievers, Birch, His, Chadwick, Domesday Book, the Liber Albus, the Lex Salica, the Vulgate…), with the full citation of each. Entries link here when they cite one.

Liebermann never printed a bibliography for the Wörterbuch: he cites by author and a short title, often abbreviated in several ways (“Birch Cart. Sax.”, “Birch Cartul.”). He did give two short lists of works elsewhere in the Gesetze: the abbreviations of the Sachglossar (II.2, p. 262) and the key to the earlier editions of each law (I, p. LIV). Every work in those lists is included here, and the note quotes Liebermann’s own entry. The full citations here are reconstructed from his short titles and the pages and dates he gives. Where he does not say which edition he used, the one current when the Wörterbuch appeared (1906) is given, and the note says so. Anything still uncertain is marked to check.
