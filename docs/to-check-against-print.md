# To check against the print

A running list of readings to check in the printed *Wörterbuch*. They come from the cross-checks against the team's Word transcriptions, where the site's scan transcription and the Word document disagree and neither is clearly right. New items are added at the end of each section as each document is checked.

**How to use it:** look the passage up on the page given, write what the print has in the last column, and tick the box. The site is changed afterwards, in one go. Until then, the site keeps the reading in the "Site" column.
